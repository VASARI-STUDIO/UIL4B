licence. The same list is data in `src/data/previewAssets.js`, and a test fails
on a file that has no entry in either place.

Credit shown on screen where a licence asks for it (CC BY, ODbL) goes in the
preview's own footer line. Unsplash and CC0 images are credited here only.
