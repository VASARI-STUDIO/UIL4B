How a job runs

Four stages, one team

The people who price the job are the people who build it. Your site manager sits in the first meeting.

Stage 01 · 2–4 weeks

Consult

Site inspection, a feasibility estimate within ±15%, and an honest read on programme risk.

Stage 02 · 6–20 weeks

Design

Design managers hold the consultants to the budget. You approve a fixed scope before we price it firm.

Stage 03 · Programme

Build

Weekly site walk, a two-page report every Friday, and one number that never moves without your signature.

Stage 04 · 12 months

Deliver

Handover with O&M manuals, a defects period of twelve months, and the site manager still answering the phone.
