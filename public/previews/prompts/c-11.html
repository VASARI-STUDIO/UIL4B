  1. Consult

    2 to 4 weeks

    A site walk, a read of the brief and the planning controls, and a straight answer on programme risk.

    You receiveA feasibility estimate within 15%, and a draft programme.

  2. Design

    6 to 10 weeks

    Our design managers run the consultants to a cost plan, so the drawings and the budget arrive together.

    You receiveA permit set and a fixed lump-sum price.

  3. Build

    30 to 90 weeks

    The site manager who priced the job runs it. The completion date is in the contract and it does not move without your signature.
