The Filey Gansey, navy

£285Knitted to order · 6 weeks

A fisherman’s jumper knitted in the round from 5-ply worsted, so tight it sheds spray. Rope cables and marriage-line diamonds across the yoke, a gusset under each arm, and your initials in the hem, the way they have been knitted on this coast since the 1850s.

Colour: Navy
Chest size: 40

Knitted by Margaret Cammish, and four others in Filey.

Margaret learned from her grandmother, who knitted for the herring fleet. The patterns are the town’s own: rope for the fisherman’s trade, diamonds for the net, and the ladder of life up the middle.

Margaret, knitter no. 1

100% British wool, 5-ply worsted spun in West Yorkshire, knitted on five steel needles at nine stitches to the inch.

  • Knitted in the round: no side seams to split
  • Underarm gussets for reach, knitted in, not sewn
  • Rope cable, marriage-line diamond and ladder panels across the yoke
  • Initials in purl stitch above the hem ribbing
  • Weight about 780 g in size 40

A gansey fits close, with about 10 cm of ease. It relaxes by roughly 2 cm with wear. If you are between sizes, take the larger.

Free UK delivery by tracked post; £14 to Europe, £24 worldwide. Ready-knitted sizes ship in 1–2 days.

Returns within 30 days for anything ready-knitted. A gansey knitted to order with your initials cannot be returned, but if it does not fit we will re-knit it once, free.

Hand-wash cool with a wool soap, press the water out in a towel, and dry flat. Never tumble dry.

Free darning for five years. Post it back with a note of where it has worn, and Margaret or one of the knitters will mend it in the same wool.
