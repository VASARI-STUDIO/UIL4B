Swell — first run

A surf-forecast app for the east coast of Australia. The flow gets someone from install to a saved break in four screens, and every screen can be skipped. Transitions between screens are horizontal swipes; the back gesture works from screen two onward.

Motion

Screens move horizontally: forward pushes left, back pulls right, 280 ms with a standard ease. The progress dots interpolate with the drag rather than snapping at the end, so a half-swipe reads as a half-step.

With Reduce Motion on, the horizontal push is replaced by a cross-fade and the dots snap. Nothing in the flow depends on an animation finishing.

Exit routes

Every screen after the first can be left: Skip on the highlights, Not now on permissions, Skip for now on the account. A user who taps past all three still lands on a working home screen set to their last-seen break.

Copy rules

No screen says “welcome”, “get started” or “you're all set” on its own. Each headline states one specific thing the app does, and each body line gives the limit as well as the benefit — the permission screen names exactly when location is read.
