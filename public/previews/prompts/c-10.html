Notes

Motion

Forward pushes left and back pulls right: 280 ms, cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1). With Reduce Motion on, the push becomes a 200 ms cross-fade, and nothing in the flow waits for an animation to finish.

Exit routes

Every screen after the first can be left: Skip on the highlights, Not now on permissions, Skip for now on the account. Someone who skips all three still lands on a working home screen set to their last-seen break.

Copy rules

No screen says “welcome”, “get started” or “you’re all set”. Each headline states one thing the app does, each body line gives the limit as well as the benefit, and the permission screen names exactly when location is read.

Swell is a fictional app, designed as an onboarding study. All forecast values are illustrative.
