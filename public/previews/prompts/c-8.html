The mark

One bean, one continuous groove, drawn with an uneven hand so it reads as made rather than generated. The horizontal lockup is the default. Use the stacked lockup only below 120 px of width.

Voice

Say the useful thing. The coffee is good enough that it does not need adjectives in front of it.

We write

“Kieni AB from Nyeri is on filter until Sunday. It is $6, and it is worth the extra dollar.”

We do not write

“Embark on a curated sensory journey with our artisanal single-origin experience.”
