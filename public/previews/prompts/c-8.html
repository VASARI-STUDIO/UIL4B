1The mark

A cup seen from above: the pour spirals out of the crema and carries on round the rim, one stroke, which is also a G. It is drawn at a 9 unit stroke on a 120 unit square and never redrawn by hand.

Clear space

Keep x clear on every side, where x is half the width of the mark. Nothing enters it: not a price, not a rule, not the edge of the sleeve.

Minimum size

The mark alone never goes below 16 px on screen or 6 mm in print. The horizontal lockup stops at 96 px or 30 mm.

3Type

4In use

Four printed pieces carry the identity. Sizes are trim sizes; the printer's bleed is 3 mm on every side and is not shown.

5Voice

Say the useful thing: what it is, where it is from, what it costs and when it runs out. The coffee is good enough that it does not need adjectives in front of it.

We write

  • Kieni AB from Nyeri is on filter until Sunday. It is $6, and it is worth the extra dollar.says the price
  • Closed Monday 6 October for the long weekend. Back Tuesday at 6.30.
  • Oat is free. So is the window bench.

We do not write

  • Embark on a curated sensory journey with our artisanal single-origin experience.
  • We will be taking a well-deserved break to recharge!
  • Plant-based milks available for a small surcharge.
