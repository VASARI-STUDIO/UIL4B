02

The measurement we will not move on

scale-fade origin center bottom · 1.0s

0.05 mm

The largest gap we will accept between a mitred tube and the joint it sits in. Anything wider and the filler has to bridge instead of wick, and a bridged joint is a joint that will crack in a decade.

Tolerance sheet · rev. 9 · every frame, every joint

03

Brazing is a temperature, not a technique

horizontal-slide alternating · 0.9s

Silver filler wicking into a head tube joint at 650 °C

650 °C

Silver, not brass

Silver flows three hundred degrees below the point where this tubing loses its heat treatment. Brass is cheaper and needs a heat the tube cannot survive.

Post-braze alignment check on the surface table

± 0.3 mm

Then we check it cold

Every joint pulls as it cools. The frame goes on the surface table twice — once after the front triangle, once after the stays — and gets cold-set until the dial indicator stops arguing.
