Billing, answered

What counts as a monitored job?
Any schedule we watch: a cron line, a Kubernetes CronJob, an Airflow DAG. Paused jobs and jobs deleted before the day ends are not counted.
What happens if I go over?
Nothing stops. Extra jobs are billed at your plan’s per-job rate, pro-rated by day, and you get an email at 80% and 100%.
Someone joins or leaves mid-month?
Seats beyond the included ten are pro-rated by day in both directions, and credited on the next invoice.
Can I switch between monthly and yearly?
Yes, at any time. Moving to yearly credits what is left of the month; moving to monthly starts when the year ends.
How can I pay?
Visa, Mastercard or Amex on Pro. Enterprise can also pay by invoice with 30-day terms, in AUD, USD or EUR.
Do you give refunds?
Within 30 days of any charge, in full, no form to fill in. After that, we refund the unused part of a yearly plan if you close the account.
