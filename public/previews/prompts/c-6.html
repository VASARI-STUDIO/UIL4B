Billing questions

Anything not covered here, email billing@cronwright.example.com — a person answers within a business day.

What counts as a monitored job?

One scheduled task with one expected schedule. A job that runs every five minutes counts once, not 288 times a day. Paused jobs don't count against your limit until you resume them.

How does per-seat billing work mid-month?

Seats are prorated to the day. Add someone on the 20th of a 30-day month and you're charged a third of a seat. Remove a seat and the remainder is credited against your next invoice — we don't refund seats to a card.

What happens if I go over the job limit?

Nothing stops working. We keep monitoring every job you've added and email the workspace owner. On Pro, jobs past 200 are billed at $0.30 each on your next invoice; on Free, new jobs stop being accepted after 14 days.

Can I move from monthly to yearly later?

Yes, at any time. The unused part of your current month is credited against the annual invoice. Moving back to monthly takes effect at your renewal date rather than immediately.

Which payment methods do you accept?

Visa, Mastercard and Amex on Free and Pro. Enterprise can pay by invoice on NET-30 terms in AUD, USD, GBP or EUR. Purchase orders are fine; send them to ap@cronwright.example.com.

Do you offer refunds?

Cancel an annual plan within the first 30 days and we refund it in full. After that the plan runs to the end of its term — we don't do partial-year refunds. We email every workspace owner 14 days before a renewal charge.
