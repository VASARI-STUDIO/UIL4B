About FlowFix

Started with one van and a 4am callout

Gavin Ruddock finished his apprenticeship in Mayfield in 2004 and went out on his own five years later, working out of a Hiace with a second-hand drain camera. The first job was a burst riser in a Hamilton terrace at four in the morning.

FlowFix is now nine plumbers and two apprentices working out of a yard in Islington. Every tradesperson on the team is licensed in their own right — we don't subcontract a job out and hope it goes well.
