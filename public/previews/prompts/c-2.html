Marisa Ferrante

Chef and co-owner

  1. 2008Apprenticed at her grandmother's trattoria outside Alba, Piedmont.
  2. 2014Moved to Melbourne. Six years on the pasta section of a Lygon Street kitchen.
  3. 2021Opened Osteria Luna with Danny Kwok, who runs the floor and the wine list.

My grandmother's trattoria outside Alba had a menu short enough to cook properly every night, and I have never wanted a longer one. We cook four dishes a course and change them when the produce changes. Pasta is rolled in the window at six each morning and never held over. When the peas finish, the risotto comes off. It is not a fast kitchen, and we are not sorry about it.

Marisa Ferrante, on the kitchen
