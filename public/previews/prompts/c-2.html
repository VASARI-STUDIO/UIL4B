Our story

We only cook what we would put on the table at home

The menu is short on purpose. Four dishes a course, changed when the produce changes, and nothing that needs a garnish to explain itself. If the pine mushrooms stop, the risotto comes off.

Pasta is made every morning by hand — tonnarelli, pappardelle and gnocchi, never held over to the next day. The ragù goes on at seven and comes off at three. It is not a fast kitchen and we are not sorry about it.

Danny runs the floor and the list. He will happily pour you something Piedmontese you have never heard of and take it back if you don't like it.
