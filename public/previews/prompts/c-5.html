What the twelve weeks look like

Four phases. Each one changes one thing, so you can tell what worked. The weights go up slowly on purpose, and two weeks are deliberately lighter.

  1. Weeks 1–4

    Foundation

    Technique on the squat, bench and deadlift, three sessions a week. We find your starting numbers and fix the thing that hurts.

  2. Weeks 5–8

    Build

    More sets, the same careful weights. Week 8 is lighter so you arrive at the next phase fresh rather than sore.

  3. Weeks 9–11

    Peak

    Fewer, heavier sets. Accessory work drops away so the main lifts get the energy.

  4. Week 12

    Test

    A new top single on each lift, filmed, and a conversation about the next block.

Before you book

The questions people ask in the consult, answered here so you don’t have to.

What does it cost, all in?

The weekly price and nothing else: gym access for our sessions is included. If you train on your own days at another gym, that membership is yours.

I haven’t trained in years. Is that a problem?

No. Most people I coach are coming back to it. Week one starts from what you can do comfortably, not from what you used to lift.

What happens if I miss a session?

Give me 24 hours and we move it within the same week. Missed without notice, the session counts. Two holiday weeks a year pause the block at no cost.

What is guaranteed?

That every session is planned, that the plan changes when you need it to, and that you can stop after the first block. What is not guaranteed is a number on the bar or the scale; anyone who guarantees that is guessing.
