About

Five years in agencies taught me what to stop doing

I ran identity teams at two Melbourne studios before going out on my own. The useful thing I took with me was a short list of what actually moves a brand: a mark that survives being 16 pixels wide, a type system somebody can use without ringing me, and words the founder is willing to say out loud.

I work alone, on one project at a time. You get me in the room for the whole engagement, not a senior for the pitch and a junior for the build. Every project ends with a guidelines file and a two-hour handover with whoever inherits it.
