About

I grew up between Kumasi and Leeds, trained as a typesetter, and spent four years in-house at a Lisbon design festival before going on my own in 2018. I work alone, on about six projects a year, and I draw most of the type myself. If a brand needs to be remembered, it usually needs one idea said clearly, and then said the same way for ten years.
