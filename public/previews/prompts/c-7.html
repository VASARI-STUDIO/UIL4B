About this property

A 1928 California bungalow on the high side of Rosella Street, held by the same family since 1994 and extended across the rear in 2019 by Kettle & Grange. The original four rooms off the central hall are intact — leadlight, picture rails, Baltic pine floors — and the new work sits behind them rather than through them.

The rear addition is a single 8.2 × 5.6 metre room facing due north, with a 3.4 metre raked ceiling and full-height glazing onto the deck. Double glazing throughout the extension, hydronic panels in the original rooms, and a 6.6 kW array that covered about two thirds of last year's electricity.

Rosella Street runs off Mackay, so through traffic is limited to residents. The 19 tram is a six-minute walk at the Moreland Road stop, and Coburg North Primary is directly across the reserve.

  • North-facing rear — the living room takes direct sun from about 9am through to mid-afternoon in winter.
  • Rear lane access to a double garage off Wilkes Lane, with room for a workshop bench.
  • Two separate living zones — the original front lounge with the gas fireplace stays a quiet room.
  • 622 m² with a genuinely usable back yard, 14 metres of lawn behind the pool fence.
  • Neighbourhood Residential Zone — the streetscape is protected, so the outlook is not going to change.
