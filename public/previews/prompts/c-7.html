The house

A Californian bungalow built in 1926, weatherboard on a red-brick base under a terracotta-tiled roof, on a 562 m² lot with 15.24 metres of frontage and rear-lane access.

The front four rooms are original, with 3.3 metre ceilings, Baltic pine floors and leadlight casements. The house was restumped in 2018. In 2019 the rear was replaced with a 7.0 by 8.8 metre kitchen, dining and living room on a slab, with a raked ceiling to 4.2 metres, a skylight and double-glazed sliding doors to a north-facing deck.

Rosella Street is a short, one-way street of similar houses, 450 metres from Coburg station and 300 metres from the Sydney Road trams. Street parking is unrestricted.
