Under a second: nothing

A loader that flashes for 300 ms reads as a glitch. Wait 400 ms before showing any of these, then keep it up for at least 600.

Known shape: skeleton

If you can draw the layout before the data arrives, draw it. A spinner in the middle of an empty panel throws the layout away.

Known fraction: ring

Only use the ring when the number is real. A ring that stalls at 99% is worse than an honest spinner.
