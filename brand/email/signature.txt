Plain-text signature, for clients set to send plain text and for the
plain-text alternative part of a multipart message. Keep it in step with
signature.html by hand: nothing generates one from the other.

Deliberately no ASCII rule, no box drawing, no quote. A plain-text part is
read by two audiences - people whose client strips HTML, and spam filters
comparing the two parts of a multipart message. A text part that differs
wildly from the HTML part is a spam signal, so this says the same four
things in the same order.
